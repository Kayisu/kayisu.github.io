## Approach

Carbon reports today are mostly built on consultant hours; the data is scattered and the work
ends at a single total. EcoReport AI is being designed to turn less input into a traceable
inventory and recommendations that fit the size of the business.

- **Less input.** Data is requested in the language of the business's own invoices.
- **Traceable inventory.** Unit conversion and factor selection happen in the system; every
  number appears in the report with its factor, line and version.
- **Recommendations by scale.** Suggestions are filtered by the business profile; each action
  shows an estimated cost and reduction, and accepted actions become a yearly plan.

## How it works

1. **Upload.** The business uploads its invoices and records as they are.
2. **Ask.** Carbon AI asks for whatever is missing in a chat.
3. **Calculate.** The calculation engine builds the inventory; the source of every number stays
   in the report.
4. **Recommend and plan.** Recommendations sized to the business and a yearly carbon plan.

The calculation engine always produces the numbers; Carbon AI asks and explains.

## Status

In development since March 2026 at the Sera Incubation Programme, Erciyes Teknopark. The
emission factor dataset (v1.3.1), an SME calculation core prototype and field research are in
place. The project was accepted to stage 1 of TÜBİTAK BİGG.

Business v1 is planned to be in use with pilot businesses by 06/2027, Carbon AI v1 and the
recommendation layer by 12/2027, and the enterprise supplier portal by 06/2028.
