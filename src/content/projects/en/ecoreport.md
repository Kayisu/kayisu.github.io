## Overview

EcoReport is a SaaS platform that helps companies produce sustainability reports
aligned with the TSRS and ESRS frameworks. It uses LLM-powered drafting to
accelerate the manual reporting workflow, allowing teams to spend less time
preparing disclosures and more time on their substance. The project is being
incubated through Erciyes Teknopark's Sera programme.

## My role

As co-founder and CTO, I lead the technical direction, including the architecture,

develops.
