## Problem

Comfort in a room is commonly described through proxies such as temperature and
humidity. Cognitive comfort—how conducive a space is to focus and ease—is harder
to capture. This thesis asks whether it can be measured.

## Approach

The project combines an IoT sensing layer with computer vision to derive a
cognitive-comfort score from the environment and its occupants. The signals,
model, and scoring method remain under investigation as part of the thesis.

## Current status

The work is in progress as a senior thesis. Its design and findings will be
documented here as the research develops.
